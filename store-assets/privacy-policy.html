Pip's Picture Pantry Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-16

Pip's Picture Pantry is a cozy picture puzzle game by Sunny Spoon Studios.

Information We Collect

Pip's Picture Pantry does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The game stores limited gameplay information on your device only, such as puzzle progress, completed cards, in-game spoon balance, unlocked stages, optional player nickname, language settings, and sound settings.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to Sunny Spoon Studios or any external server.

Children

Pip's Picture Pantry is designed as a gentle puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Advertising and Analytics

Pip's Picture Pantry does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

The Sunny Spoon Studios website uses Google Analytics only after a visitor chooses Allow analytics. Website analytics may collect limited usage information such as page visits, approximate region, device and browser type, language choice, and clicks on store, support, YouTube, email, refund-help, and privacy-policy links. This information helps us understand website use and improve our public information. It is not combined with gameplay progress or in-app purchase records.

Visitors can decline analytics or change their choice at any time using Privacy choices in the website footer. When analytics is declined, the website does not load the Google Analytics tag. Google processes consented website analytics data under its own terms and privacy policy.

In-App Purchases

Pip's Picture Pantry includes optional in-app support purchases for app-store processed spoon support and top-up items. Payments are processed by the official app store platform. The app does not collect, store, or receive your payment card details. Store product identifiers for release tracking: pip_cozy_support, pip_spoon_jar_small.

Data Sharing

We do not share gameplay data with third parties. If a website visitor allows analytics, limited website usage data is processed by Google Analytics as described above. We do not sell personal information.

Data Deletion

Because gameplay data is stored only on your device, you can delete it by using the reset option in the app or by uninstalling the app.

Website visitors can withdraw future analytics consent through Privacy choices in the website footer and can clear existing browser storage or cookies through their browser settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. The updated policy will be posted at the same public URL.
